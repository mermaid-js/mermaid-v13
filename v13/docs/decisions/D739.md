# D739: The radar title moves up when it meets an axis label; amends D354

The radar title stays where v12 put it, middle-anchored on the centre at the canvas top with `dominant-baseline: hanging`, unless its box meets an axis label's box. Then it moves up until its bottom is `titleLabelGap` (4 px, `packages/diagram-radar/src/layout/metrics.ts`) above that label's top, and again until it meets none. Its `x` stays. The bounds grow to cover it (D354), so the `viewBox` may start at a negative `y`.

The title's whole box counts, every line of a title with `<br>`. Boxes that only touch do not meet, as for the legend (D614).

The cause is D355: v12 printed `<br>` literally, so the top label (`auto` baseline) was one line. In v13 its extra lines grow up from that baseline and reached the title (#1257, and the two-line title in its comment). A one-line top label does not reach the title with the default config and theme, so those charts, and every chart where nothing meets, are byte-identical. No existing fixture changed.

The layout invariants (`layout/invariants.ts`) gain a rule: the title meets no axis label.

D354 said "v12's positions stay"; for the title this now holds only while nothing meets it, as D614 already did for the legend.
