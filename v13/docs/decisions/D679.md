# D679: C4 relations reach a person's body, and labelled row neighbours stand a tag apart; amends D454

**Proposed by the PR that adds this file, for the maintainer's confirmation.**

Classic C4's grid and router (D454, D455) change in three ways, so that a relation beside a person ends on its body and every relation label (an edge-label tag, D626) can sit on its own line:

1. **A person's body is its port.** A person drawn as a figure offers its body, not its whole box, to the lines that leave or reach it from the side: the straight line between two shapes, the room repeated lines share (`pairRoom`) and the ports on its left and right sides are taken on the body. Ends are still clipped to the real outline, and a line from above still ends on the head.
2. **A row holding a person lines up on the body.** v12 and D454 top-aligned every row, so a shape beside a person stood level with its head. A row (TB, BT) that holds a person now lines up the middle of each member's port: the person's body, any other shape's own middle. Rows without a person stay top-aligned. LR and RL columns are unchanged.
3. **Labelled row neighbours stand far enough apart for their tags (amends D454).** D454 put elements `2·c4ShapeMargin` apart. Two neighbours in one row joined by labelled relations now stand at least the widest tag between them apart, plus at each end the 10 px arrowhead, the 6 px `tagClear` beyond it (the room a tag keeps from a shape, as much as its 6 px corner radius) and 1 px: 17 px from the end of the line, so a tag stands 7 px clear of every arrowhead there, its own or a neighbouring line's. Where their lines are closer than a tag but at least half a tag apart, the tags are staggered: each sits on its line at one end, alternately at one shape and the other, and the gap holds the widest tag at each end side by side, 6 px apart. Where they are closer still, the tags go where they find room and are marked `crowded`. Shapes in different boundaries keep the gaps the boundaries give them.

Lines between two shapes keep each tag's own height and 2 px either side from the next where the room allows, so a two-line tag (a label with its `[techn]`) is not crowded by its neighbours. A tag the placement leaves off the line it belongs on (one of several between two shapes), or with its own line running through it off its middle, is marked `crowded`, so the checks list it. A tag the author moved with `UpdateRelStyle` offsets stays where the author put it (D455) and is never marked.

For a v12 user: a diagram with a person in a row draws its other shapes in that row lower, level with the person's body, and two shapes joined by a long relation label stand further apart. Nothing changes for text output (`format: 'ascii'`), where a person is a box, or for c4-beta, which is laid out by `layered`.

40 classic fixtures draw differently (25 of them in `pr-check` and `baseline`): 25 wider by 3 to 185 px (5 of those also 29 to 99 px lower), one 1 px lower, and 14 the same size, with their rows lined up on a body.

Rejected: growing the shorter shape to hold every tag (shapes keep their size from their text, D446), and bending the lines so their tags spread.
