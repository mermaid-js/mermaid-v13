# D730: Gantt's tinted section bands alternate like zebra rows, and their contrast is measured as drawn

In the classic family, the third section band (`sectionBkgColor2`) now repeats the first (`sectionBkgColor`) in the light rules and in the dark rules of gantt's theme table. The tinted rows then alternate with `altSectionBkgColor` like zebra rows, all at one strength. `altSectionBkgColor` is unchanged, including `dark`'s white alternate rows (D667).

Gantt draws every band at `bandOpacity`, 0.2 (`layout/metrics.ts`, as v12 does). D726 checked the raw colours. Over `background` as drawn, the first band was 1.11 to 1.19:1, and the third band, still `mainBkg`, was 1.00 to 1.07:1 (`base` 1.00, `default` 1.03), so it barely showed. That was Knut's finding in the Argos review of #1365.

The contrast is now measured as drawn: each band is composited at 0.2 over `background` before taking the ratio. `packages/conformance/src/gantt/theme.spec.ts` checks every classic theme for the following:

- each tinted band is at least 1.1:1 from the page;
- band 3 is band 1;
- the grid lines and the done bars are at least 1.2:1 from the band as drawn;
- `taskTextOutsideColor` and `titleColor` reach AA on it.

As drawn, the tinted bands are `default` 1.19, `neutral` 1.15, `forest` 1.17, `base` 1.11 and `dark` 1.15:1 from the page. The grid and done bars are 1.23:1 or more from the band, and the text is 9:1 or more.

Two of D726's raw-colour checks are dropped:

- **Band 1 against band 3.** They are one colour now.
- **The plain and active bar fills against the band.** Those bars carry an outline. Their fill sits no further from the page than from the band: in `base` the plain bar is 1.01:1 from the page. So the check measured the bar, not the band.

D726's ratio table was measured on raw colours. The ratios above replace it as the measure of what is seen.

The `DIFFERS` rows for `sectionBkgColor2` follow: `default` `#9898d1` (v12 `#fff400`), `neutral` `#aeaeae` (v12 `#bdbdbd`), `forest` `#98b25c` (v12 `#6eaa49`), `dark` `#000000` (v12 `#eae8d9`), and a new row for `base`, `#d1bb8b` (v12 `#fff4dd`). An author's `sectionBkgColor` now moves both tinted bands, and `sectionBkgColor2` still sets the third on its own.

Amends D726.

(Knut, 2026-11-27.)
