# D760: Which diagram types take a `curve` key

D733 gives `<type>.curve` > `curve` > the type's default to every diagram type that draws edges, and stops where a type needs more than passing the curve name through. This decision settles the types that stopped.

**Implemented:**

- **Usecase:** `usecase.curve` > `curve` > S-curves, as merged in #1379. The layout's routing follows the curve, as it does for flowchart, class and state. Its text output warns `ASCII_DROPPED` (curve) when a curve is set, as flowchart, agentflow and requirement do.
- **Eventmodeling:** `eventmodeling.curve` > `curve` > today's look (#1380), on three conditions:
  - with nothing set, today's routes are built as before and the output is byte-identical;
  - with a curve set, a frame relation is drawn with the shared `routePath`, and its head takes its angle and trim from the drawn path;
  - self relations are unchanged.

**No key:**

- **ER**, for now. ER draws its own paths, and its crow's feet are placed from the route's legs. A follow-up ticket moves ER onto the shared `routePath`, with an ER default that keeps today's output, and then adds `er.curve`.
- **Architecture:** heads, rotated labels and line hops depend on its straight or single-bend lines.
- **Block:** ends are clipped along a straight line.
- **C4:** it has its own router and paths (D455).
- **Cynefin:** a transition is one bow, with no route to bend.
- **Mindmap:** its basis drawing follows the ports, and no shared curve draws it. There is no follow-up.
- **Wardley:** its straight links carry meaning on the map.

**Not edges in D733's sense,** so they take no key:

- gitgraph (lanes and arcs);
- sankey (flow bands);
- ishikawa (bones);
- treeview (guides);
- railroad (tracks);
- sequence (messages);
- gantt (no edges);
- timeline and journey (guides);
- radar and the chart types (data series).

Amends D733.
