# D685: Icon frames leave room around the glyph

A flowchart icon shape's frame leaves room around its glyph (#1150). A `square` or `rounded` frame is the glyph's square plus `flowchart.padding` (15 by default, so 7.5 px a side), with rounded corners of radius 5, as v12's `iconSquare` and `iconRounded`. A `circle` frame's diameter is the glyph's diagonal plus `flowchart.padding`, about 83 px around a 48 px glyph; v12 drew the diagonal plus 40, about 108 px. The glyph is centred in the frame, the label sits 8 px from the frame, as in v12, and edges end on the frame. An icon without a `form` keeps its 6 px label gap and its box.

The circle differs from v12 on purpose. This extends D298, which settled the icon shapes' paint, to their geometry.

(Knut, 2026-11-23, spec #1163, decision 5.)
