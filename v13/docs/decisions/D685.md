# D685: Icon frames leave room around the glyph

A flowchart icon shape's frame leaves room around its glyph (#1150). A `square` or `rounded` frame is the glyph's square plus half of `flowchart.padding` (15 by default, so 3.75 px a side; 55.5 px around a 48 px glyph), with rounded corners of radius 5. A `circle` frame's diameter is the glyph's diagonal plus half of `flowchart.padding`, about 75 px around a 48 px glyph. The glyph is centred in the frame, the label sits 8 px from the frame, as in v12, and edges end on the frame. An icon without a `form` keeps its 6 px label gap and its box.

v12's `iconSquare` and `iconRounded` add all of `flowchart.padding`, and `iconCircle` draws the diagonal plus 40, about 108 px. v13's frames are tighter on purpose. Spec #1163's decision 5 took v12's padding; after review of the first version (#1180) the room was halved. This extends D298, which settled the icon shapes' paint, to their geometry.

(Knut, 2026-11-23, spec #1163, decision 5, amended in review of #1180.)
