# D662: ER relationship labels keep the theme's `fontSize`; v12's fixed 14 px is a listed difference

v12 draws and measures ER relationship labels at 14 px in every theme and under every look, because its `er/styles.ts` has `.edgeLabel .label { font-size: 14px; }` and it measures the label in the DOM with that rule applied. No other v12 diagram type has such a rule. v13 draws and measures them at the theme's `fontSize`, as every other label: the label takes the root's font size, and `diagram-er/src/layout/prepare.ts` measures it with `theme.number('fontSize')`. So with no override a v12 label is 14 px and a v13 label 16 px, and under `themeVariables.fontSize: '18px'` v12 stays at 14 px while v13 follows to 18 px (#1042 findings, row 2b).

v13 keeps the theme's `fontSize`, so that one font setting sizes the text of every diagram type alike. The difference from v12 is recorded in MIGRATION.md's ER SVG structure section. Classic themes carry v12's classic geometry (D152), but a font size is not a geometry variable, and the label tag around the text is D626 to D628's. The other two ER rows of the findings need no decision: the relationship line and its ends take `lineColor` in both versions, as classic markers do (D575), and the grey line under v12's label is its translucent label box showing through; the zero circle is white, as v12 `look: classic` draws it (D152, D153).

Rejected: drawing and measuring ER relationship labels at 14 px in every theme, as v12 does and as D493 keeps the Ishikawa head label at 14 px. It would shrink the label box and the layout in about 187 of the 235 ER fixtures (every one with a non-empty relationship label), in every theme, and make ER the one type whose labels ignore the theme's `fontSize`.

(Knut, 2026-11-19, spec #1035, #1042.)
