# D743: Event modeling relations between lanes leave a box from distinct points and go around boxes they would meet

D521 kept v12's one segment for a relation between lanes, from ⅔ of the source's width to ⅓ of the target's, with nothing in the way checked. Every relation that left one side of a box left from the same point, and a segment ran through or along whatever box lay between, striking out its name (#1234). In docs-008, D521's placement put the reset frame CartItems under ItemAdded, so the relation from ItemAdded to OtherUI ran up CartItems' left edge from the point where ItemAdded's relation to CartItems left, and read as leaving CartItems.

Two changes, both in the event modeling layout:

1. **Distinct points.** The relations that leave one side of a box are ordered by where they meet their targets. The first leaves at v12's ⅔; the others follow to its right, ⅓ of the width divided by one more than their number apart, so none reaches the corner. A box with one relation leaving a side draws it as before. Ends that arrive at a box keep v12's ⅓.
2. **Detours.** A relation between lanes whose segment enters a box other than its two, or comes within 5 px of one, is redrawn from vertical and horizontal pieces. It goes up or down at an x that is 10 px clear of every box and lane label of the lanes between its two (the column). Where the column meets the source's or the target's edge, at least 10 px in from its corners, the relation leaves or enters there; otherwise it leaves at its anchor, runs across the 10 px gap between its lane and the next, and turns into the column there. Fewer jogs win, then the shortest run across. Detours whose runs would share a line take the gap's middle, then 8 px away from the target, then 8 px toward it; columns stay 8 px apart, and a moved end keeps 8 px from the other ends on its side. If no detour fits, the segment stays.

Relations that clear every box by 5 px and are the only one leaving their side are byte-identical to before. The layout invariants now check every relation, not only those within a lane, for entering a box other than its two: a box's name and data lie inside it, so no relation strikes one out.

v12 drew most of these crossings too; the issue's five cases (docs-008, the themes family's CartItems→CheckoutScreen, and three probes) all clear their boxes now.

Rejected: distinct anchors only, which fixes docs-008 but leaves the other four crossings; S-curves or diagonals around boxes, which would need a general router.

Amends D521.
