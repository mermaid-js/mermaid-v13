# D764: Kanban text wraps inside the label inset on both sides

Card text (title, ticket and assignee) wraps at `W − cardInset − 2·labelInset`: 165 px at the default `sectionWidth` of 200, where it wrapped at `W − 25` (175 px). The card is still `W − cardInset` wide and its text still starts `labelInset` (10 px) in from its left edge, so every line now ends at least `labelInset` inside the card's right edge, as v12 drew it, at the default width and at `sectionWidth: 300` alike (#1294). In the metrics table `wrapInset` goes from 25 to 35 px.

Column titles get a side inset too: they wrap at `W − 2·labelInset` (180 px at the default width), where they wrapped at `W`, and are centred inside that inset, in line with the card text. In the table `titleInset` goes from 0 to 20 px. The room under a column title (`titleRoom`, #1175) is measured as before and does not change.

The least `sectionWidth` (`MIN_SECTION_WIDTH`, `wrapInset + minWrap`) becomes 36 px, where it was 26. A `sectionWidth` above 0 and below it is raised to it, with `KANBAN_CONFIG_IGNORED`, as before.

D647's fit rule is restated from the new wrap: a ticket and an assignee fit side by side when their widths add up to less than the card's width less `labelInset` at each side, which is now the wrap itself (165 px at the default width). The rule's result is unchanged.

The text output keeps its cell table (`KANBAN_CELL_METRICS`), which already left a cell's inset on both sides of card text (`wrapInset` 8 = `cardInset` 4 + 2 × `labelInset` 2) and three cells either side of a column title; its output does not change.

The layout invariants check it: card and column text keeps `labelInset` on both sides of its box, in px and in cells.

Amends D372 (`W − 25` for cards, `W` for column titles) and restates D647.

(Knut, 2026-11-27, spec #1339, #1294.)
