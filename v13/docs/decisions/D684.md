# D684: A track keeps an arrowhead's last leg from the node

Under stepped routing, the layered engine keeps each track `trackMargin` from the ranks on either side of its gap, and a gap that holds tracks grows to make room for that. In the px profile `trackMargin` goes from 18 (Pele's value) to 24.5.

With 18, the step off a label's track turned 18 px from the node: the plain-flowchart case of #1160, a 6 px jog and an 18 px last leg. That is just enough for the classic themes' arrowhead, which trims 7 px (7 + 6 + 5 = 18). The redux and neo arrowhead trims 13.5 px, so the line was trimmed into the second corner and came into the head still turning. PR #1179 fixed the same bug under ELK; this is ticket B10, #1186.

24.5 is the last leg an arrowhead needs after a bend, the value #1179 gives ELK (`LAST_LEG` in `layout-elk`):

- 13.5: the trim under the largest point arrowhead, the redux and neo themes' (`markerTrim`: gap 4 + length 10.5 − 1);
- 6: the straight `LEAD` the renderer draws before a marker;
- 5: one full corner of the `rounded` curve.

It is one constant for every theme, not read from the theme, so the layout does not move with the arrowhead (D154). The classic themes keep a few px more than their arrowhead needs. The margin applies at both ends of a gap, since the tracks in one gap serve edges that point either way. The cell profile keeps its `trackMargin` of 2.

Where tracks make a gap grow, the other gap of the same rank of labels and bends grows by the same amount, so a label stays where D682 puts it between its nodes. Without this, a relationship label with a marker at both ends sat 9 px off the middle in ER. This is for tracks only, and not on the grid.

(Knut, 2026-11-23, spec #1163 decision 12: the layered engine leaves room for the largest arrowhead on the last leg.)
