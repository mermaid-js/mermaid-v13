# D722: Under ELK, a box too small for the corner margins sits centred on its lone ends

#1303 is fixed only if its offset measures 0.5 px or more; below that the issue is closed with the measurement. It measured 4 px: in `stateDiagram-v2` `[*] --> [*]` under ELK (fixture `state-diagram-v2/nightly-check/elk/elk-start-end-edge-centering`), both 14 px nodes were centred at x = 15 and the edge ran straight down at x = 19, at every point. So it is fixed.

The cause is ELK's corner margin, `elk.spacing.portsSurrounding` = 12 at each end of a side (v12's `PORTS_SURROUNDING`, set on the root and on every group). On a side shorter than the two margins together ELK cannot keep both, and it puts a lone end `12 - 1` = 11 px from the side's start, whatever the node's `portAlignment` (measured with elkjs 0.12: 11 px for every side from 6 to 22 px, the centre from 23 px on). Its node placement then lines that end up with the far end, so a start state above a wider state sat 4 px beside an edge that met the wider state at its centre. ELK reads the margin from the parent only, so it cannot be lowered for one node.

Under ELK layered, the adapter therefore moves such a box onto its ends after layout: when every end on a box is alone on its side, those sides are parallel and shorter than the two margins, and all the ends are the same distance off centre, the box moves by that distance. The edges stay where ELK routed them, so an edge from a start state to a wider state runs through both centres. A box with two or more ends on one side (ELK spreads those about the centre), with a pinned port (`FIXED_POS`), with a self-loop (D719) or with an edge ELK did not route stays where it is. So does a group, whose children and title would not move with its frame, and a box with an end label on one of its edges, which ELK placed beside the box. It runs whether `elk.straightenEdges` is on or off, after the step at the ends is straightened. The diagram keeps ELK's size, so `[*] --> [*]` is 4 px wider than before, with the room on the left.

v12's runtime draws the case centred (the issue's comparison). The v12 develop adapter has the same margin, and the conformance oracle installs no v12 ELK plugin, so v12's pixels were not measured here. Parity under ELK is with v12's options (D174). This is a correction after layout for one case where ELK does not honour those options, like the straightening of the step at an edge's ends.

Refines D174.

(Knut, 2026-11-26, spec #1335, decision 6.)
