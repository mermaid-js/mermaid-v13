# D683: Six flowchart shapes take their v12 look back

Six flowchart shapes are drawn as v12 drew them (#1152, ticket #1167):

- **browser**: a rectangle rounded 12, at least 90 wide, with v12's `padding` (15) round the label. An 18 px bar at the top holds three dots of radius 2.5 at 12 + 9i from the left, filled in `nodeBorder` with no stroke, and an address bar (10 high, rounded 3, at 0.6 opacity) stroked in `nodeBorder`. The label is centred below the bar.
- **console**: the same window with a 20 px band at the top and no divider. The prompt `>_` is a `<text>` element in monospace, bold, 14 px, filled `nodeBorder`, 12 in from the left. The SVG writer allows `text` and the font attributes (R-SEC-2), and D5 rules out only HTML labels, so the glyph is real text rather than strokes.
- **cloud**: v12's ten arcs round the label and its `padding` (the frame), which bulge out of the frame. The node is the box the arcs cover, and the label stays at the frame's centre, as in mindmap (spec #294 T4). Mindmap's `cloudOutline` moves to `@mermaid-js/shared/shapes/outline`, which both types import; mindmap's cloud is unchanged byte for byte. Boxes of one size hold clouds of up to three frames, so the size passes its frame to the drawer and the edge ends (`ShapeSize.frame`). Edge ends spread along the cloud's sides and stop on the arcs.
- **flag**: v12's `waveRectangle`. The body is the label plus `padding` high and the label plus twice `padding` wide; its top and bottom are each one sine cycle an eighth of that height deep, the top right bulging up and the bottom left down, so the node is 1.5 times the body's height. Edge ends follow the waves.
- **fork** and **join**: hollow, with square corners, filled `mainBkg` and stroked like any outline (the gradient under `neo`). Its size, 70 × 10, and its turn to 10 × 70 in LR and RL stay (D604).
- **fr-circ**: a ring 14 across round one 5 across, both filled `mainBkg` and stroked like the outline.

v12's shape code filled the fork and the fr-circ inner circle, but its flowchart stylesheet (`.node rect, .node circle { fill: mainBkg }`) drew them hollow, and that is what v12 flowcharts showed. This amends D185, whose "`fr-circ` inner dot" is now a hollow ring that takes the gradient, and the migration guide's fork "filled in plain `nodeBorder`" (D185). The look is the same in every theme (D152); v12 `look: neo`'s fill of the fork was not checked. State diagrams keep their own filled fork, join and end state.

(Knut, 2026-11-23, spec #1163, decision 4.)
