# D683: Browser, cloud and console take their v12 look back

Three flowchart shapes are drawn as v12 drew them at `63333fd8db` (#1152, ticket #1167):

- **browser**: a rectangle rounded 12, at least 90 wide, with v12's `padding` (15) round the label. An 18 px bar at the top holds three dots of radius 2.5 at 12 + 9i from the left, filled in `nodeBorder` with no stroke, and an address bar (10 high, rounded 3, at 0.6 opacity) stroked in `nodeBorder`. The label is centred below the bar.
- **console**: the same window with a 20 px band at the top and no divider. The prompt `>_` is a `<text>` element in monospace, bold, 14 px, filled `nodeBorder`, 12 in from the left. The SVG writer allows `text` and the font attributes (R-SEC-2), and D5 rules out only HTML labels, so the glyph is real text rather than strokes.
- **cloud**: v12's ten arcs round the label and its `padding` (the frame), which bulge out of the frame. The node is the box the arcs cover, and the label stays at the frame's centre, as in mindmap (spec #294 T4). Mindmap's `cloudOutline` moves to `@mermaid-js/shared/shapes/outline`, which both types import; mindmap's cloud is unchanged byte for byte. Boxes of one size hold clouds of up to three frames, so the size passes its frame to the drawer and the edge ends (`ShapeSize.frame`). Edge ends spread along the cloud's sides and stop on the arcs.

The look is the same in every theme (D152). `flag`, `fork`/`join` and `fr-circ`, which #1152 also named, stay as v13 draws them: the ticket was narrowed to these three, so spec #1163's decision 4 (a hollow fork and fr-circ) is not carried out, and D185 and D604 stand as they are.
