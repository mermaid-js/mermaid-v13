# D723: Message and frame text follow v12's vertical placement

A sequence diagram places the text of messages and frames where v12 drew it on the page. Only the text moves: every message line, row height, frame border and the diagram's height stay where they are, and text output (cells) does not change. Before this, v13 used v12's text y as the top of the label box. v12 used it as a middle baseline (`dominant-baseline: middle`, `dy="1em"`), so v13's text sat about 10 px higher than v12's.

## Message labels (#1237)

v12 drew a message's text with its middle 1 em plus half of `wrapPadding` below `starty + 10`, and placed the line from rows it measured in Arial. Measured in Chromium with the conformance oracle's mermaid 12.2.0, at 16 px, the last line's alphabetic baseline sits 10.81 px above the line for every arrow and for a curved self message, in `default` and `redux` alike (10.86). With `rightAngles` it sits 0.81 px above a self message's line, which v12 draws a `boxMargin` higher.

v13 puts a message label's last baseline `messageFontSize + boxMargin.y − 10 − wrapPadding / 2` above its line, with no `boxMargin.y` for a self message with `rightAngles`. That is 11 px at the defaults, within 0.2 px of v12. The distance does not depend on the font or on the number of lines, so a multi-line label keeps the same gap under its last line. v12 drew each line of a multi-line label one rendered line height (19 px in trebuchet) apart over rows 18 px tall, so its gap shrank by 1 px per extra line; v13 does not copy that.

v12's distance moved with the size of the Arial rows it measured (rounded to whole pixels), so it does not scale evenly: 11.34 px at 14 px and 20.72 px at 24 px, against v13's 9 and 19. v13 keeps one rule that matches v12 at its default size. The `redux` themes draw messages at 14 px, where the gap is 2.3 px less than v12 at the same size.
