# D647: A kanban card stacks a ticket and an assignee that do not fit side by side

When a kanban card has a ticket and an assignee that do not fit side by side on one row, the SVG draws the ticket on one row and the assignee on the row below, still right-aligned at the card's right inset. They do not fit when their widths, each wrapped at `W − 25` as before, add up to at least the card's width less the 10 px inset at each side (165 px at the default `sectionWidth` of 200). This is the rule the text output (#466) already uses.

A card with stacked rows grows to hold them. The two rows are rows of their own, as in the text output: the card grows by the ticket's and the assignee's full heights, the title stays 10 px below the card's top, and the assignee ends 10 px above its bottom. The column and the cards under it move down by what the card grew.

A ticket and an assignee that fit keep v12's shared row and v12's card height, and a card with only one of them is unchanged.

v12 drew the two on the same row whatever their widths, so long ones were drawn over each other and ran past the card's bottom. This is a deliberate difference from v12.

(Knut, 2026-11-19, spec #996, #774.)
