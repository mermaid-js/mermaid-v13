# D765: A kanban word wider than its wrap breaks

In px, a word wider than the wrap is split between characters (`keepWords: false` in core's `layoutLabel`, its default), in card titles, tickets, assignees and column titles. Text never crosses its card's or column's right edge. Under D372 such a word stayed whole and overflowed its box, as in v12 (#1304, F-008).

The text output already split such a word, so its output does not change. A single character wider than the room between the insets, which only a `sectionWidth` near its least can leave, still overflows: no break keeps it inside.

This is a deliberate difference from v12, listed in `packages/conformance/divergences/kanban.json` and `v13/docs/migration/220-kanban.md`. `e2e/diagrams/kanban/nightly-check/long-words.mmd` shows it.

Amends D372.

(Knut, 2026-11-27, spec #1339, #1304.)
