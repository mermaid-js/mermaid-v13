# D719: ELK honours `LayoutGraph.metrics.loop` for self-loops

Under ELK layered, a self-loop reaches `LayoutGraph.metrics.loop` out from its node before it turns, and its label sits `metrics.loopGap` past the loop, as under `layered` (D77). The ELK adapter sets both on each node or group that has a self-loop, through ELK's individual spacing (`spacing.nodeSelfLoop` and `spacing.edgeLabel`). A diagram type that asks for more room gets it under ELK too: ER asks for 42 px so that both cardinality marks fit before the turn (`erMetrics`), the others get the px profile's 26 px. Without `metrics` the px profile's values apply.

The two ends of a loop are spread to the corner margins of their side (ELK's `portAlignment` `JUSTIFIED` on the sides across the flow), not a third of the side apart, so the marks or arrowheads at the two ends keep apart. This applies to every diagram type laid out with ELK layered. A graph with no self-loop is laid out as before, and the other ELK algorithms (`elk.stress`, `elk.mrtree`, …) are unchanged.

v12 left the loop to ELK's defaults (10 px out from the node), where an ER self-relationship's marks overlapped and shrank (#1233). Parity under ELK is with v12's options, not its pixels (D174); this decision adds two options v12 did not set, for self-loops only.

Several self-loops on one node stay nested on one side, as ELK draws them; only the outermost reaches the corner margins.

Refines D77 (the engine's loop length comes from the unit profile) to cover the ELK engine, and D174.

(Knut, 2026-11-26, spec #1335, decision 3.)
