# D729: A gantt task label is placed inside its bar only when it fits the bar as drawn

Provisional, pending Knut. Chosen while Knut was offline; he can reverse it (see below).

With `excludes`, a task has two ends: `endTime`, which keeps counting the excluded days, and `renderEndTime`, where the drawn bar stops. v12's renderer reads `endTime` to choose the label's side (the class `taskText`, `taskTextOutsideRight` or `taskTextOutsideLeft`) and `renderEndTime` to place it (its `x`). When the label is wider than the drawn bar but not wider than `endTime`'s span, v12 therefore calls it inside, with the centred anchor, and puts it at the bar's end plus 5 px. The label is then centred on a point just past the bar's end, over the bar (#1249).

The case in #1249's diagram, "Implement parser and jison" (`after des1, 2d` under `excludes weekdays 2014-01-10`), at the default config: label width 132.66, `end` (`endTime` − start) 185, `renderEnd` (`renderEndTime` − start) 123. The bar spans 199 to 322, the label 260.7 to 393.3, so about 61 px of text lies on the bar and the rest hangs past it. v13's `labelOf` copied the rule, so it drew the same overlap as v12 (checked in `ganttRenderer.js` at fa796a943: the class reads `d.endTime`, the `x` reads `d.renderEndTime || d.endTime`).

The side now follows the drawn width, as the position already did. A label wider than the bar goes right of the bar, or left when that would pass the chart's width (v12's rule, unchanged). A label that fits the drawn bar stays inside and unchanged. Milestones (a square `barHeight` wide) and `vert` markers (their label is never on the marker) keep their rule. A new layout invariant, in `layout/invariants.ts`, says an `inside` label is no wider than its bar. The conformance layout corpus runs it over every gantt fixture.

In the visual fixtures four change: `nightly-check/handle-multiline-section-titles-with-different-line-breaks` and three in `pr-check/` whose names start with `should-render-a-gantt-chart` or `should-render-a-gantt-diagram-with-data-labels`; all four draw this diagram.

Alternative, not chosen: keep v12's overlap and list it as a known difference. It leaves a label drawn across a bar's end, in the inside text colour, and no one wrote the rule down as a choice. Switching to it costs about one revert commit: the change is the `classEnd` choice in `labelOf` (`layout/bars.ts`), the invariant, their specs and this file.

A difference from v12 on purpose: listed in the gantt migration fragment (`v13/docs/migration/200-gantt-chart.md`).
