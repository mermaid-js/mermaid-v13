# D734: A sequence frame's tab keeps its width and the frame grows to clear it

A sequence frame's label tab is as wide as its keyword needs, never narrower than `labelBoxWidth`. v12 fixed the tab at `labelBoxWidth` and let a long keyword (`critical` at 16 px in the `default` theme) poke out of it. v13 keeps the tab that grows with its keyword, and the frame and its title follow the tab: the frame's minimum width is the title's width plus the tab's width, and the title is centred right of the tab, instead of using `labelBoxWidth` for both. A title never overlaps its tab, for every keyword and theme.

The frame is wider than v12's only when the tab is wider than `labelBoxWidth`, by the excess: about 10.6 px for `critical` at 16 px in `default` (a 60.64 px tab against 50), none at the `redux` themes' 14 px. Before this, the title sat where a 50 px tab would have ended and overlapped the wider tab by about 10 px (#1361). The first draft of this decision said 1.6 px, which read the tab's right edge (51.64) as its width.

(Knut, 2026-11-27, #1361.)
