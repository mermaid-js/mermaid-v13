# D704: The front matter is read after leading `%%` comment and directive lines

Before the opening `---`, the front-matter scanner (`scanFrontMatter`, DESIGN §4.1) skips whole lines that are blank, a `%%` comment, or one `%%{…}%%` directive closed on the same line with nothing after it. The opening fence is then looked for as before, and the front matter's `title` and `config` apply. Nothing is rewritten: the front matter's range starts at the fence's line, the body starts after the closing fence, and every range and diagnostic is an offset into the source as written. A fence that is never closed after such lines gives `UNCLOSED_FRONT_MATTER`, as it does without them. Any other text before the `---`, or a directive that is not closed on its line, means there is no front matter, as before.

Before, the scanner looked for the fence only on the first non-blank line. A `%%` line made the `---` the first text of the body, and detection rejected every diagram type with "Diagrams beginning with --- are not valid…" (#1216, #1204, #1211, #1236).

This differs from v12 on purpose. v12's fence regex is anchored at the start of the text, so it does not match; v12 then removes comments and directives, and the Langium types draw the diagram without the front matter's `title` and `config`. The author's settings are kept here instead of being dropped without a warning. The difference is listed in MIGRATION "Front matter and directives" and in the front-matter fence divergences (`packages/core/src/preprocess/divergences.spec.ts`).

Extends D642, which skips a byte order mark before the fence in the same way. Directives on the skipped lines still apply: D705.

Rejected: matching v12, drawing the diagram and ignoring its front matter.

(Knut, 2026-11-26, spec #1331.)
