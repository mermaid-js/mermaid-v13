# D659: Event modeling routes a relation that skips a box of its lane under the row, and hangs the self loop under the box; amends D521

D521 runs a relation within a lane from the source's right edge to the target's left edge, at each box's middle. When a box of the lane sits between the two, that line runs through it and strikes out its name, and it lies on the same line as the relations to and from that box (#672). D521 also gave a self relation a loop over the box's top right corner, 10 wide and 10 high, so most of the loop was arrowhead.

A relation to the next box of its lane keeps D521's route. A relation that skips one or more boxes of its lane is drawn under the row instead: down from the source's bottom, along a channel 25 px below the lowest box bottom it spans, and up into the target's bottom. Relations whose spans overlap take channels 10 px apart, shorter spans higher. The ends on one box spread evenly between the ⅓ and ⅔ anchors of the relations between lanes, a single end at ½. They are ordered so that no leg crosses the run of a shallower channel.

A self loop hangs under the box's bottom right corner, 20 wide and 25 deep, with its right leg 10 in from the right edge. A relation that runs past the loop takes a channel 10 px below it.

Only a lane that holds such a relation or loop grows, to 15 px below its lowest channel or loop. Every box keeps v12's top, 15 below its lane's top, and the lanes below a grown lane move down by as much. Relations between lanes keep v12's segment from anchor to anchor. Event lanes have busy top edges (up to read models, in from commands) and free bottoms, which is why the routes go under the row.

The layout invariants now also check that no relation within a lane enters a box other than its two ends, that no two relations share a run, that a relation within a lane stays inside it, and that each leg of a self loop is at least twice the head long. A relation between lanes may still cross a box, as in v12, so it is left out of the first check.

Rejected: drawing the run over the row inside today's 15 px of lane padding, where the head takes most of the last leg and only about two channels fit; and only enlarging the self loop, which leaves the struck-out name.

(Knut, 2026-11-19, spec #995 decision 1, #672.)
