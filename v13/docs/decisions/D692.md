# D692: A self-loop's room is kept on the side it is drawn on; amends D618

**Proposed by the PR that adds this file, for the maintainer's confirmation.**

`layered` used to grow the room laid out for a node with a self-loop by the loop and its label twice, once on each side, although the loop is drawn on one side only. Each node with a loop therefore had an empty band as wide as its loop on the side without one, and a neighbour on that side stood a loop's width further away than the node spacing (#1173). An ER entity with one labelled loop drew 362 px wide where 251 px hold it; v12 drew 187.

Off the grid, a loop's room is now kept on the side `selfLoop` draws it on, and only there. Following D618, the first loop of a node and every other one after it go on the near side, which is the right in TB and BT and below in LR and RL. The others go on the far side. The node's room grows by each loop's room on that loop's side. The middle of the room moves off the node's middle by half the difference between the two sides (its *bias*), so edges still meet the node's middle and the loops are drawn where they were. D618's "in the room already reserved for them" now means each side's own room. A node with one loop on each side has the same room on both.

Two cases keep the room on both sides, as before:

- the loop of a group, a composite state's among them (state `transitions/two-self-loops`, `composites/edges-to-composites`), so the loop stays inside the group's parent;
- every node on the grid (`format: 'ascii'`), whose loops take cells by slot.

A straight port line that leaves the side of an item in a group (`sideEnd`) still starts at the side of a room centred on the item; no source reaches that path today.

For a v12 user, a diagram with a self-loop is narrower in TB and BT, and shorter in LR and RL, by about the loop and its label. The node now has only the diagram's margin on its free side.

Rejected: trimming only the empty band at the drawing's edge, which leaves the extra gap to a neighbour, and putting the loop on whichever side has room, which would move loops that D618 places.

(Proposed 2026-11-24 in the PR that adds this file, for the maintainer's confirmation. #1173.)
