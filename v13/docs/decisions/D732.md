# D732: The gap below a composite state's title strip equals `groupPad`

In a titled composite state, the room between the title strip line and the top of the nearest child (start dot or state) equals `groupPad`, 20 px, the padding on the other three sides. All four inner sides are even, as #1307 asks, rather than v12's 8 to 12 px. It holds at every nesting level. The fix is in diagram-state: core's layered placement is unchanged. Composites split into regions keep their dividers running border to border, and untitled composites are unchanged.

(Knut, 2026-11-27, spec #1338, #1301, #1307.)
