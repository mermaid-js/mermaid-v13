# D671: The core budget rises from 104,448 B to 104,576 B gz (+128 B) for the own-member edge fix; amends D670

The `core` budget in `packages/mermaid/size-budget.json` (R-PERF-8: the `mermaid` entry's static import closure on the CDN build's ESM entry, gzip level 9) rises by 128 B, from 104,448 B to 104,576 B (102.125 KiB), for the fix of mermaid-js/mermaid#8824 (#1060): under `layered`, a labelled edge from a group to its own member was drawn on top of another edge into the member's top.

| | core, B gz |
| --- | --- |
| On `mermaid-v13` at `f5aac064a` | 104,318 |
| The fix (`freeAcross` in `layout/layered/compound-own.ts`) | +133 |
| Sum | 104,451 |
| New limit | 104,576 |

The figures are `cdn-build.spec.ts`, the larger of the two measures that enforce the core budget; `core-size.spec.ts` measures 104,213 B with the fix. D670 left 130 B of margin, 3 B short. The new limit is the first 128 B step that leaves at least 64 B of margin: 125 B.

The fix was first written with a search for the nearest free place on the member's top (+358 B) and then reduced to steps of one port pitch to the right, then to the left. Trimming it further cost the tolerance of its comparisons and still left it at the limit with no margin.

The `loaded` limit (R-PERF-1) does not change.

As D546, D599, D625, D662 and D670 do, the raise is a decision, not a gate loosened to make a change pass. Rejected: trimming core elsewhere to make room, which would mean unrelated changes in a bug fix.

(Knut, 2026-11-20, #1060.)
