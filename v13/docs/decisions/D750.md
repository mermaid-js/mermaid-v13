# D750: A venn area's text nodes start below the labels and are pulled inside the area as far as they fit; amends D613

**The grid starts below the label.** In an area with a placed label (a set's own id counts as its label), the text grid starts `labelTextGap` (`10s`, `metrics.ts`) below that label, one-line labels included. D613 moved the grid only by what a label of two or three lines reached below one line, so a one-line label still lay on the first row of text nodes (`A` over `React`, `Vegetables` over `Carrot`, #1295). If one of its text nodes would then meet another area's label, it starts `10s` below that one too. Only the nodes' boxes count, not the whole grid: an empty cell, or the room between boxes, may lie on a label (`layout/text-grid-corner-near-label`). The grid never starts higher than v12's.

**The grid is pulled inside the area as far as it fits.** Each row is as high as its tallest text node, divided by the cell factor (`0.9`). The grid is centred on the area's inner circle. It starts at v12's width (`1.9 · r`) and narrows to the inner circle's width at its edge farther from the centre. Narrower boxes wrap the nodes onto more lines, so this takes up to `gridPasses` (8) passes. It stops narrowing when a node would no longer fit its box. When it does not stop early, every cell is inside the inner circle. Keeping the nodes off the label wins over keeping them inside the circle: in a squeezed area (a small circle under a tall label), the grid is not stopped at the circle's bottom, and its lower nodes may sit below the circle. v12's grid was a `1.9 · r` square about the centre, and a grid moved down under a long label ran past the circle.

**Fewer columns when a word does not fit.** v12 used `⌈√n⌉` columns. A grid that D750 moves uses fewer columns when the widest word of any node would not fit its box at v12's width, and one column when even that does not fit. A word wider than its cell ran into its neighbours (`REST` into `GraphQL` in a thin overlap, in v12 too).

**Areas without a label.** An overlap with no label keeps v12's grid, unless one of its text nodes would meet a label. Such a grid is then laid out as above, starting below the labels its nodes met (`Terraform` under the three-set overlap's `Everything` in the `themes/*` fixtures).

**Invariant.** `layout/invariants.ts` checks that no text node's label meets a set or overlap label, its own area's or a neighbour's.

**Divergence from v12.** v12 drew these overlaps too. The text nodes of every labelled area now sit lower than v12 drew them, by the label's height plus `10s` or more. Where an area has no room below its label (a thin overlap), its text nodes can still reach past its outline, as they could in v12. The row in the venn section of MIGRATION.md (`v13/docs/migration/350-venn-diagram.md`) describes the change.

Not part of this decision, and tracked in #1449: text nodes of different areas meeting one another when neither area has a label to clear, and nodes in thin regions that clear their label but cross the region's outline. No invariant checks that cells stay inside the inner circle; the layout promises only that the nodes start below the label and are pulled inside as far as they fit.
