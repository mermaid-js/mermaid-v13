# D678: The rect-grow helper is in core/layout

The helper that grows a rectangle by the same amount on every side, step C-106 of spec #1102, is `growRect` in `@mermaid-js/core/layout`, not in `@mermaid-js/shared/layout/geometry`. This replaces spec #1102's decision for C-106. Its decision for C-117 and C-118 is unchanged.

A new shared subpath is a chunk of its own, so cynefin, eventmodeling, ishikawa, kanban, treemap and treeview, the six diagrams that import the helper, would each grow 87 to 101 B gzip. In core, the six diagrams' static closures shrink by 11 to 27 B each, 142 B over the whole build, and core grows by 30 B.

(Ashish, 2026-11-20, spec #1102.)
