- Not expressible as `LayoutModel` points (a hop is an arc on the path). In v13 it is a `postRender` edit on `SvgTree` (`d` attr) by the package, or a T0 proposal (spec E4). Needs edge polylines recoverable from the tree — v13 paths carry no `data-points`.
