## Exports (main entry)

- `JISON_NAMES`: kind → `agentflow.jison` terminal (dev/test only).

## Token facts the parser needs (oracle-checked)

- The text is v12's `withComments`: comments **kept**, no trim (leading blank lines and comments are tokens before `GRAPH`), directives cut (trivia), `;` drops, and the blanks between `}` and its line break dropped (`drop` holes; they belong to no token, unlike a dropped `;`, which the token before it covers).
- `T.Comment` = `[ \t]*%%(?!\{)[^\n]*` outside labels: blanks before it included, significant (v12's grammar reads `COMMENT`). Earlier rules win: `%% direction LR` is `direction_lr`, `a%% c` is `NODE_STRING a%%`, `%%x@y` starts a `LINK_ID`. `%%` inside text/ellipse/trapezoid labels is `Kind.Comment` trivia to the line break; the label's `TEXT` goes on after it on the next line.
- `SPACE` is one blank a token. Links: `LINK` `\s*--+[x>]\s*` and `\s*-.+-\s*`, `START_LINK` `\s*--\s*` (edge text closes only with `--+[x>]`). `---`, `--o`, `-.->` lex as START_LINK/LINK plus text; `==`, `~` and `=` are `Kind.Error`.
- Keywords: `flow`, `connector`, `class`… end at jison's `\b` (`flow-x` is `flow` + `-x`); `global` and `end` take trailing `[ \t]*`. `agentflow-beta` opens `dir` once.
- D463: a lone `%` in a text or trapezoid label is `TEXT` (12.2.0 errors); `%%{` there is still an error.
