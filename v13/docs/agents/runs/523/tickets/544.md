## Exports (main entry)

- `AgentflowTokenKind` (`src/parser/tokens.ts` `T`): flowchart's kinds less `Subgraph`, plus `Flow`, `Connector`, `Global`, `Comment`; `Graph` is `agentflow-beta`. `ShapeDataEnd` and `ClickKeyword` have no jison name (as flowchart's).
- `JISON_NAMES`: kind → `agentflow.jison` terminal (dev/test only).
- `agentflowTokens(src, maxTextSize = 50_000)` → `AgentflowToken[] { kind, text, range }`: significant tokens (directives and in-label comments left out), `text` through core's `cook`.
- Internal for #546: `lex(src, bodyStart, view, graphKeyword?, probe?)` (`lexer.ts`, same signature as flowchart's) over `agentflowSourceView(src, bodyStart, directiveRanges, maxTextSize?)` (`source.ts`; pass the site config's `maxTextSize`); read labels with core's `cook(src, start, end, view)`. `scanner.ts` holds the shared scans (split only for `max-lines`).

## Token facts the parser needs (oracle-checked)

- The text is v12's `withComments`: comments **kept**, no trim (leading blank lines and comments are tokens before `GRAPH`), directives cut (trivia), `;` drops, and the blanks between `}` and its line break dropped (`drop` holes; they belong to no token, unlike a dropped `;`, which the token before it covers). Past `maxTextSize` (measured as v12 did: CRLF as LF, directives out) it is v12's `cleaned`: line comments are `Kind.Comment` holes and the leading blanks a `drop` hole.
- `T.Comment` = `[ \t]*%%(?!\{)[^\n]*` outside labels: blanks before it included, significant (v12's grammar reads `COMMENT`). Earlier rules win: `%% direction LR` is `direction_lr`, `a%% c` is `NODE_STRING a%%`, `%%x@y` starts a `LINK_ID`. `%%` inside text/ellipse/trapezoid labels is `Kind.Comment` trivia to the line break; the label's `TEXT` goes on after it on the next line.
- `SPACE` is one blank a token. Links: `LINK` `\s*--+[x>]\s*` and `\s*-.+-\s*`, `START_LINK` `\s*--\s*` (edge text closes only with `--+[x>]`). `---`, `--o`, `-.->` lex as START_LINK/LINK plus text; `==`, `~` and `=` are `Kind.Error`.
- Keywords: `flow`, `connector`, `class`… end at jison's `\b` (`flow-x` is `flow` + `-x`); `global` and `end` take trailing `[ \t]*`. `agentflow-beta` opens `dir` once.
- D463: a lone `%` in a text or trapezoid label is `TEXT` (12.2.0 errors); `%%{` there is still an error.

## Decisions and unmet

- Choice: a package-local source view instead of a core `SourceViewOptions` flag (GAPS preferred it; no core bytes).
- Choice: the view also reproduces v12's `maxTextSize` fallback to the comment-free text (the padded `agentflow-max-text-size` case needs it for parity).
- Choice: top-level comments are a diagram kind (`T.Comment`), not `Kind.Comment`, since v12's grammar consumes them; in-label ones are trivia.
- The 8 in-tree v12 conformance fixtures are lexer cases only; T2 ports them as `.mmd` with their expectations. The SHARED_SYNTAX parity spec (D467) is #546's. Unmet: none.
