## Behaviour

- Completion: statement keywords by the probe (`end` only in a block), directions at the header and after `direction`, the three links with their semantic, edge-text closers (`-->`, `--x`), used ids (kind and label as detail; flows as `subgraph`), classes after `:::`/`class`, `id[id]` and `id@{ shape: <alias> }` after a used id; in `@{ … }` keys by owner (node `shape`/`label`/`labelType`, flow `view`, edge `animate`/`animation`/`curve`) and values (shape names, the six aliases first; `collapsed`; `text`/`string`/`markdown`).
- Hover: keyword docs (`accDescr {` and its `}` too); a node's kind, label, drawn shape, classes, flow, the collapsed flow it is drawn in, metadata, edge counts; a flow's title, members, view, direction, metadata; a link's and an edge id's semantic and ends; a `shape:` name's kind and drawing (`SHAPE_REMOVED`/`SHAPE_UNSUPPORTED` named).
- Folding: `flow`/`global … end`, multi-line `@{ … }` (chain, connector), `accDescr { … }` (the parser's statement spans). Diagnostics: D461's three codes at their ranges (golden `diagnostics/shapes`).
