## Exports

- Main entry: `layoutAgentflow(model, ctx)` → `AgentflowLayout` (`LayoutModel` + `agentflow: AgentflowPrepared`: `graph`, `groups` (frames, outermost first), `nodes`, `edges`, `views`, `pads`, `metrics`, `titleMargin`). `ctx` (`AgentflowLayoutContext`): `config`, `measurer`, `theme`, `warn`, optional `units`, `layout` (default `layered`), `inline`; the pipeline's `RenderContext` fits. Also `prepareAgentflow`, `shapeOutline(shape, w, h, pads, metrics)`, `AGENTFLOW_CONFIG_IGNORED`, view types.
- `./testing` (new): `agentflowLayoutProblems(model, layout)`: drawn/hidden per v12, nesting as the model says, each edge kept/redirected/dropped by v12's rules, views' sizes, every end on a node on its outline. Use with core's `validateLayout(layout.agentflow.graph, layout)`.
- The plugin (#550) passes its `RenderContext` as `ctx`; `layout: (m, ctx) => layoutAgentflow(m, ctx)`.

## Rules

- Collapsed flows are `collapsedGroup` nodes; hidden ids are skipped; edges redirect to the outermost collapsed flow; a fold of two distinct ends into one card is dropped; an authored self-loop stays (flowchart drops it). An edge with an end drawn nowhere (`connectors`) is left out.
- Flows are `LayoutGroup`s: `parent` from the model, own `direction` (`inheritDir` applied by the parser), `algorithm` passed through (`mermaid` warns `ELK_ALGORITHM_IGNORED` under ELK, D174), title room + `flowchart.subGraphTitleMargin`.
- Sizes: label wrapped at `agentflow.wrappingWidth`, widened to `minNodeWidth` (not the card), shape padding from the theme and `flowchart.padding`; spacing and `diagramPadding` from `agentflow`. Defaults in `metrics.ts` until `SECTION_OWNERS` (#550).
