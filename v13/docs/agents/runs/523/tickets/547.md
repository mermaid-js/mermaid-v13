## Rules

- Collapsed flows are `collapsedGroup` nodes; hidden ids are skipped; edges redirect to the outermost collapsed flow; a fold of two distinct ends into one card (a member's edge to its own collapsed flow included) is dropped; an authored self-loop stays (flowchart drops it). An edge with an end drawn nowhere (`connectors`) is left out. Ends: on node outlines and frame borders; a member-to-own-flow edge runs straight out to the nearest frame side.
- Flows are `LayoutGroup`s: `parent` from the model, own `direction` (`inheritDir` applied by the parser), `algorithm` passed through (`mermaid` warns `ELK_ALGORITHM_IGNORED` under ELK, D174), title room + `flowchart.subGraphTitleMargin`.
- Sizes: label wrapped at `agentflow.wrappingWidth`, widened to `minNodeWidth` (not the card), shape padding from the theme and `flowchart.padding`; spacing and `diagramPadding` from `agentflow`. Defaults in `metrics.ts` until `SECTION_OWNERS` (#550).
