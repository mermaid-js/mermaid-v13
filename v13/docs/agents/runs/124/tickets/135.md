## Behaviour
- `isValidId`: entity/subgraph names must read back alone on a line as one `entity` symbol; keywords (any case: `End`) and cardinality/line words (`one`, `many`, `to`) are refused with a reason. Classes as `classDef <name> fill:#000` reads them.
- Completions: keywords by the kinds the parser tests; left/right cardinality symbols by the token before (a line or not); `--`/`..`; `:::`; entities and subgraphs (alias as detail); classes after `:::`, in `class A |`, `classDef |`; `TB/BT/LR/RL` after `direction `; in a block `PK/FK/UK` after a name and the document's types at a line start; nothing in values, labels, aliases, comments, directives, front matter.
- Hover: entity (alias, attributes, relationship count, classes), subgraph (title, members, direction), class (`classDef`, users), cardinality/line (meaning + the relationship it sits in), `PK/FK/UK`, keywords from `erDocs`.
- Lexer: `lex(src, pre, probe?)` reports the jison start condition (`INITIAL block blockBq accTitle accDescr accDescrMultiline style`) as `probe.mode` (the GAPS item from #131 is closed).
