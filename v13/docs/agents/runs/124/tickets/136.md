## Drawing
Tables from `EntityView` bands (name band, rule, dividers, cells `nodePad.x` in). Each end: a tee on the border, the near mark (bar, foot or MD parent) in the end cell and the outer mark (bar or ring) in the cell before it, also on a corner. Dotted lines for non-identifying. Group frames with the title on the border; an end on its own group's frame faces inward. Front-matter title centred on line 1.
