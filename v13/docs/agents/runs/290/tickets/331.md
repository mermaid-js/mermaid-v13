## What the facet does

- **Parse:** T2's parse plus `section`/`task` symbols (names trimmed, first `def`) and section blocks (a section's span holds the statements up to the next section line) for folding. The plugin's parse is unchanged.
- **Parser change (probe only, additive):** `Probe` + `ProbeCursor` when `options.probe` is set; `at(Journey)` in the header, `tested(statement kinds)` per statement start, `at(TaskData)` after a task name; lexer modes were already recorded.
- **Completion:** keywords where tested; actors after a task's second `:`. **Hover:** actor, section, task, score (D226 faces), keyword docs. **Rename:** actors across tasks, sections and tasks by name; read-back refuses `,` `:` `#`, keywords, `%` starts.
