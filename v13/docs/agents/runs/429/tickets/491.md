# #491 T3 layout and venn.js solver: handoff

Branch `feature/491_venn-layout`. No renderer, plugin export, manifest or registry row (T4, T5). The parser's `VENN_INVALID_SIZE` also covers sizes too large to square (`src/sizes.ts`). Choices are in `GAPS.md` under #491.

## Exports

- `@mermaid-js/diagram-venn`: `layoutVenn(model, ctx)` → `VennLayout`. `ctx` holds `config`, `measurer`, `theme`, and optionally `inline` and `warn`. It reads `config.venn.{width, height, padding, useDebugLayout}` and `fontFamily`, and warns `VENN_ZERO_SET`. Also: `vennStyles(model)` (v12's `buildStyleByKey`), `areaKey(sets)`, `VENN_METRICS` (the px table at 1600, `× scale`), `VENN_SLOTS`, `vennLayoutConfig`, `VENN_CONFIG_DEFAULTS` (the schema's) and `vennPluginDefaults` (drops `padding`, for T5's `configDefaults`).
- `VennLayout` fields:
  - `width`, `height`, `scale`, `titleHeight`, `padding`, and `offset` = `(0, titleHeight)`: areas, text areas and debug shapes are in this chart frame; `title` and `bounds` are in the diagram's;
  - `areas`, in v12's order (model subsets, repeats, then added pairs with no `ast`): `kind`, `sets`, `size`, `path {start, arcs[{radius, large, sweep, to}]}`, `centre`, `label`; sets also have `circle {x, y, radius, strokeWidth}`, `index` and `slot`;
  - `textAreas` (`sets`, `inner {x, y, radius}`, `grid`, `nodes[{ast, id, cell, box, label}]`), `title`, `debug` (`useDebugLayout`), `fonts` (48s, 40s, 32 px), `bounds`.
- `@mermaid-js/diagram-venn/testing`: `vennLayoutViolations`, `parsedVenn(src)` (the model, the diagnostics and the front-matter `venn` config), `vennTestContext`, `vennTestTheme`, `seededRandom`.
- For T4: draw paths with `PathBuilder.arcTo` from `path` and label boxes with `w.label`; `slot` gives `venn-set-<slot>`, `index` the palette; a text node's colour is in `vennStyles(model).get(areaKey([id]))`.

## Solver (`src/solver/`, D411, D413)

venn.js 2.0.0 is ported (`circles`, `fmin`, `solve`, `normalize`, `regions`, `index`) with mermaid's options only; mulberry32 (`random.ts`) replaces `Math.random`. Licence: `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` (venn.js MIT, fmin BSD-3-Clause), in `"files"`; `UPSTREAMS` and `REQUIRED_CREDITS` in `licenses.ts`.
