## #512 T6 language facet
- **Choice (for the maintainer):** the facet runs in the shared suites (goldens, property, rename round-trip, completion validity, code-point ranges) through a test-only `UNLISTED_FACETS` row, as radar (#450) and kanban (#463) did. Loading it lists `xychart` in the empty-text completion golden of the 13 other types (+1 line each, at the registry row's place, the line T5's manifest gives too). The coordinator's brief put that change in T5; without the row the ticket's goldens and corpus specs cannot run xychart. Undo: drop the row, `vitest run packages/language-service -u`.
- **Choice (for the maintainer):** D389's "adds quotes when a bare name gains a blank" has no carrier in the facet contract, so `LanguageFacet` gains an optional `renameText(name, kind)` (`core/src/parser-kit/language.ts`, a type: no runtime bytes) and the service's rename writes it for bare occurrences (`language-service/src/symbols.ts`; the read-back check now shifts by each edit's own length change). xychart writes a name quoted when bare text does not read back as it (blanks, punctuation, a keyword such as `bar`).
- **Choice:** categories are `standalone` (D389: each its own definition, no clash on a repeated name); plots are one symbol per name (D389 gives them only a namespace), so two plots named `Sales` rename together and renaming onto another plot's name is refused as a clash.
- **Choice:** completion offers `xychart` (not `xychart-beta`) as the header; statements only below a header and at a line start or after `;` (the grammar also takes them after `xychart` on its line, and a text without a header is not detected).
- T2 output changes: diagnostics are in source order (the model's warnings came last); `XYCHART_NO_PLOT` is zero-width at the header's end, not over the header (same line), so the completion inserting `xychart` leaves no error in its own text (`completion-validity.spec.ts`).
- Limits: a bare category of several words (`jan feb`, read `janfeb`) or a quoted one with blanks at its ends has no occurrence the service keeps (its range is not its name): no definition, references or rename there. A line ending in a blank joins the next one (v12's `\s+` takes the line break in the axis and plot states), so completion at `x-axis Month ¦` reads the next line as more of that axis, as v12 parses it.
- **Unmet (R-LANG-4, owner: coordinator):** on a 50,031-character chart (156 plots of 60 values), the bare parse takes 15–17 ms best and a cycle (update, every query, two renames) about 80 ms median; flowchart's 50k fixture took 31 ms (parse 4.7 ms) in the same run. *Not measured on a steady machine* (load ~12). The lexer is 10 ms of the parse (each position tries the state's rules in order); a first-character dispatch in the T1 lexer, re-checked by its parity gate, is the likely fix. Not a ticket criterion.
