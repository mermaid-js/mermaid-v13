# #593 T0a: swimlane engine skeleton, lane rules, phases through coordinates

Branch `feature/593_swimlane-engine` (own PR into `mermaid-v13`).

## Exports
- `@mermaid-js/core/layout/swimlane` → `swimlane: LayoutEngine` (`packages/core/src/layout/swimlane/index.ts`), not re-exported from `./layout`. `mermaid` loads it lazily (`LAZY_LAYOUTS` row); `PEER_LAYOUTS` in `registry.ts` (D502/D535) exempts it from D285's gate, `tree` stays gated. Compass T0 (#590) adds `'compass'` to the same set.
- Inputs: top-level groups are lanes (source order across the flow), loose nodes go to `__swimlane_default__` (first lane, listed in `model.groups`), nested groups are boxes grown by their own `LayoutGroup.padding`. Spacing from `graph.spacing` (`node`, `rank`); tuning from `ctx.config.swimlane` (`ignoreCrossLaneEdges` true, `optimizeRanksByCrossings` true, `automaticLaneOrdering` false). `diagramPadding` honoured; `availableWidth`, `minLen`, group `direction` unused; `grid` throws a RangeError (text output lays out with `layered`, D179; `ascii-api.spec.ts` shows it).
- Title band (D536) reserved inside each lane: top for TB/BT (21px or the title's height), left for LR/RL (36px or title height + 15). `LayoutModelGroup.title` is the band's centre. RL/BT keep the band left/top (v12 mirrored it: the listed fix).
- Edges: placeholder right-angled elbows (`placeholderRoutes.ts`), ends on the boxes; `label` = the label node's centre; `startLabel`/`endLabel` beside the end segments (`endLabels.ts`, a px copy of layered's placement). An empty nested group is laid out as a node the size of its padding and title.

## Internal shape for T0b (`layoutData.ts`)
v12 `LayoutData` subset. `nodes: Node[]` (groups listed last-first like v12's flowDb, then nodes, then `edge-label-*` nodes): `id`, `isGroup`, `parentId`, `x`/`y` centres, `width`/`height`, `padding` (v12 number = side padding), `paddingBox`, `titleSize`, `isEdgeLabel`, `isDummy`, `edgeStart`/`edgeEnd`, `layer`/`order`, `groupTitleRect`, `swimlaneContentTop`, `direction`, `shape` (`swimlane` on lanes). `edges: Edge[]`: `id`, `start`, `end`, `label?: Size`, `labelNodeId`, `isLayoutOnly`, `points?`, `curve?`. `direction`; `laneOrder` (written by the core: lanes across the flow, source or automatic order); `config.flowchart.{nodeSpacing,rankSpacing}`; `config.swimlane`. Synthetic ids hold a `\0` (`\0edge-label\0<edge>`, `<edge>\0to-label`, `\0placeholder\0N`).
- T0b: call `routeEdgesOrthogonal(data, direction)` in `layoutCore.ts` before `postProcessSwimlaneLayout` (TB coordinates, as v12), and extend `postProcessing.ts` (it holds only the direction transforms now). `adapter.ts` `routes()` already uses `edge.points` when present (axes and ports inferred) and falls back to placeholders. `toLayoutModel` places empty lanes after post-processing and shifts points with them.

## Decisions / gaps
- **Choice:** an empty lane gets a band of its own (title width + 2 × padding across, the other lanes' extent along) between its neighbours in `laneOrder`; v12 collapses it to 0 × 0, which `validateLayout` rejects.
- **Choice:** nested groups use their `LayoutGroup.padding` per side (title room on top); v12 split one number. `nestedGroupSizing` expects 2 × padding.
- **Choice:** two `eslint-disable-next-line max-lines-per-function, complexity` on v12's `assignCoordinates` and `computeSubtreeCrossCounts`, kept whole for hand-porting (D532).
- Not ported: pools/anchored cases (D531); shared-node lint (a `LayoutNode` has one parent, so it cannot happen); `look`/colour slot of the default lane (T2); "one definition of a lane" source scan (core specs have no Node APIs).
- **Choice:** a labelled edge with a group end puts its label in the ranked end's row, beside that lane's nodes (v12: on top of the other end).
- Open for T2: a lane title wider than its lane overflows (no width reserved for it); warnings `SWIMLANE_*` are not emitted.
