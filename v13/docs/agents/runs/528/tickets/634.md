## Fixes (each marked `v13 (#634)` / `v13 (#636)` in the code, D532)
- **#636** `direction/materializedGeometry.ts` `swapDestinationTerminalTailsToReduceCrossings`:
  `pathHasNodeHit` exempted the edge's own nodes on every leg; the destination is now exempt on
  the last leg only (as `terminalGeometry.ts`'s `clearOfNodes` already does). With that swap
  refused, the back edge and its forward twin (Dep1.Proc2→Dep2.Proc2) doubled back across each
  other (3 new `routeTouches` entries), so `direction/alignedEdgeStraightening.ts` gained
  `straightenOpposedPair`: two opposed edges between aligned cross-lane nodes, alone on the facing
  faces, become two straight lines `MIN_ARROWHEAD_SPACING` apart about the centre line, when clear
  of nodes, labels and other edges (not for diamond/hexagon faces). Unit spec in
  `alignedEdgeStraightening.spec.ts`.
- **#634** `direction/materializedGeometry.ts` `liftRailsOffForeignEdgeLabels`, called in
  `postProcessing.ts`'s `finalizeRenderedEdges` after `liftObstacleHuggingSameSideRails` (labels
  are anchored by then; the router leaves labels out by design): an interior rail through or
  along (2px) another edge's label moves to the label's nearer side, `ROUTE_TOUCH_TOLERANCE` (6px)
  out, if it hits no node or label, shares no track and adds no crossing. 6px rather than
  `EDGE_GAP` (12): at 12 the I→K label re-anchored onto the lengthened first leg, at I's corner.
  Unit spec in `direction.materializedGeometry.spec.ts`.
