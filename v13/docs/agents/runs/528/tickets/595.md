# #595 T0b: swimlane orthogonal router, post-processing and DDLT gates

Branch `feature/595_swimlane-router` (own PR into `mermaid-v13`; T0a #628 has merged).

## What landed
- Ported verbatim-ish from upstream `c9b12739f` (D532): `orthogonalRouter/router.ts`, `postProcessing.ts` (all passes), every `direction/*.ts` (validation, alignedEdgeStraightening, terminalGeometry, terminalLegAlignment included). No anchored nodes/pools (D531), no logger (`validateSwimlanesLayout` returns issues; not called in production). File-level `eslint-disable` of the readability rules only (D532), types tightened for `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`.
- `layoutCore.ts`: `routeForDirection` → post-processing → `routeSelfLoops` → `reattachTerminals` → `fitLanesToRoutes`. Adapter `routes()` reads `edge.points` (deduped); an edge between a node and a group around it, or one left without two distinct points, falls back to T0a's `placeholderRoutes.ts` (never throws). `LayoutNode.shape` passes through.
- v13-only files: `turnedRouting.ts` (LR and RL route on the drawing transposed, y stretched as `lrTransform` will, boxes at drawn size; v12 used unturned sizes and turned only LR's obstacle extents, so RL detours used the TB width: upstream bug too), `selfLoops.ts` (GAPS item closed: a labelled self-loop leaves the side facing its label and its far leg runs through the label's centre; the label moves to the node's centre line only if nothing is there, else the loop reaches it; unlabelled loops take a free face across the flow, then in, then out, clear of nodes, labels and routes), `terminalReattach.ts` (ends left off their box get a stub, corners included; a slide only along a leg leaving the box), `laneFit.ts` (lanes grow so routes and edge labels keep 10px inside; flowchart's 20px group padding put v12's 20px-out rails on the lane border; ends on a lane, and their copies, move with its border).
- Router/passes accept flowchart v13's canonical shapes (`diam`, `hex`); title-band passes follow D536 (BT lifts a top band, RL shifts a left band).
