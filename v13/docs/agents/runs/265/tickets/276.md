# #276 T5: per-render fixed costs (handoff)

Branch `feature/276_fixed-costs`, cut from `feature/265_render-speed` at `bdad2fc01`, the integration branch after T4 and a sync with `mermaid-v13`. PR #419 into the integration branch.

## What changed

- **Size from the writer:** `StreamSvgWriter.rootViewBox` is the root `<svg>`'s `viewBox` as written. `drawDiagram` matches it against `VIEW_BOX_VALUE`, the old regex's value part, instead of running `VIEW_BOX` over the whole SVG. The hooks path keeps `VIEW_BOX`.
- **Detection** (`registry.ts`): `---` is tested without `toLowerCase`, and "error" only lowercases a trimmed source of length 5.
- **Theme parts** (`theme/overridden.ts`): parts built from `themeVariables`, with no extra rules, are kept per spec and dark spec, by a key of the override values. Only strings, finite numbers, booleans, null and lists of those make a key; anything else is built afresh. At most 16 keys per spec. A hit gives its `THEME_VARIABLE_DROPPED` warnings again.
- **Analysis cache** (`text-cache.ts`, moved out of `api.ts` for `max-lines`):
  - a source of 16,384 characters or more is keyed by its length plus an FNV hash of 256 sampled characters and the last 64;
  - a hit compares the whole text;
  - `render()` hands `prepareWith`'s analysis to `draw`.

Specs: `writer.spec.ts` (the root viewBox) failed on the base. The others pass on the base by design: `theme.spec.ts` (warnings on a second render, no stale theme), `api-caches.spec.ts` (long sources told apart wherever they differ, a render parses once) and `api.spec.ts` (`error`, an indented `---`). Three deliberate breakages turned them red: no warning replay, a hash without the tail, no text compare on a hit.

Checks: identity against `bdad2fc01` 163 of 163; `pnpm check` green; core 94,147 B of D314's 95,232 B.

## Notes

- The regex was 2.5% of a Node render of the huge tier, and is gone from the head's profile. A `+=`-built SVG is flattened whenever someone first reads it as a whole, so part of the saving may move to the caller (`innerHTML`, a file write) instead of disappearing.
- The cache key matters in the bench, whose `%% run N` copies share one length. Node runs without the suffix don't show it.
