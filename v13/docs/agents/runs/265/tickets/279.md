## What changed

- **`measure` (D203):**
  - `layoutFlowchart` brackets `prepare` as `measure`;
  - `core/timing` leaves a stage that runs inside another out of the outer one's time, so `layout` is the engine alone;
  - other types keep an empty column.
- **Render and browser split (D204):** `renderOnce` times `render()` and insert + `getBBox` apart, and the sampler reports them per warm sample (`takeParts`). The report adds `renderMs`/`browserMs` per fixture and Σ render / Σ browser per renderer. A sample is timed as before.
- **R-PERF-10 (D206):** gate `pele-ratchet` compares v13's warm geomean against Pele with `LIMITS.peleRatio` (1.45×, recorded before #265) × 1.05. It is enforced wherever `speed` is, so it is report-only on CI (`BENCH_REPORT_ONLY=speed`, D150) without a workflow change.
- **Page history:** `driveBrowser({ pageHistory })` times perf/huge1, medium4 and medium5 again. It times them on the v13 and Pele pages that rendered the corpus, and in a fresh page each. The report prints them as "Page history".

Specs, written first and each red on the base: `timing/index.spec.ts`, `sampler.spec.ts`, `gates.spec.ts`, `markdown.spec.ts`, `interleave.spec.ts` (the `finish` hook). `dev/watch.spec.ts` now expects `measure` too. `pnpm check` green; core 94,692 B.
