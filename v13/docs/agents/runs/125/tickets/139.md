## Exports (`@mermaid-js/diagram-sequence`)
- `parseSequence(src, { preprocessed?, keepTokens?, probe?, maxDepth? })` → `ParseResult<SequenceModel, SequenceDocument>`. Never throws.
- `detectSequence(src)`; AST types (`src/parser/ast.ts`): `Participant`, `ParticipantRef`, `Message`, `Note`, `Activation`, `Fragment`/`Section`, `Rect`, `Box`, `Autonumber`, `Title`/`AccTitle`/`AccDescr`, `Links`/`Link`/`Properties`/`Details` (menu data on the node, D105), `Header`.
- Ids: `p:A` (first mention, declaration or reference), `p:A@n`; `m:A>B`, `m:A>B#n`; `note:n`; `f:<kind>#n`; `box:n`; `st:n`. Symbols: kind `participant`, first mention `def`. Statement spans nest blocks and sections.
- Model: adopted `src/model.ts`; additive `links`/`properties` on participants (menus merged as v12, reset on a labelled redeclaration); header note: activations add no participant (as v12), and `activate`/`deactivate`/`destroy` take `st:` ids, not `p:`. `autonumber N` → `{start N, step 1}`; `createAst`/`createdBy`/`destroyAst`/`destroyedBy` filled.

## How it works
- `parser/parser.ts` (rules, frames for blocks, no recursion), `rules.ts` (token access, errors, recovery), `tables.ts`, `builder.ts` (v12 `SequenceDB.apply` with its checks as error diagnostics), `normalise.ts` (`parseMessage`, `parseBoxData` with `parseColor` instead of `CSS.supports`, menu JSON, `@{…}` YAML).
- D114 done: core `sourceView`/`cook`; `parser/source.ts` deleted. The lexer keeps two sequence-only parts (its own commit): it reads CRLF as two LFs and steps the single `\s` after `title` over a CRLF; lone CR as LF; entity placeholders. Lexer parity 100%.
- Grammar `grammar/sequence.ebnf` (keywords via `(?i:…)`), wired in `tools/gen-grammar/src/generate.ts`; generated categories/docs/highlighters/`v13/docs/syntax/sequence.md`.

## Decisions taken (oracle-backed)
- Messages after a participant's destroying message: v12 accepts, so v13 accepts with warning `SEQUENCE_AFTER_DESTROY`; the layout's span invariant reports them (spec in conformance).
- Other warnings: `SEQUENCE_MATH` (`$$…$$` in messages, notes, aliases, fragment/section labels, box titles; text kept), `SEQUENCE_PARTICIPANT_TYPE` (unknown `@{type}` → participant; v12 drew nothing, bridged in the v12 normaliser), `SEQUENCE_MENU_DATA`.
- Oracle env (`conformance/src/sequence/oracle-env.ts`): v12's `parseBoxData` and `addDetails` read the DOM and threw in Node; patched on the prototype (box colour via jsdom's `@asamuzakjp/css-color` plus system colours, independent of v13's browser-rule check; `details` as in a page without the element).
