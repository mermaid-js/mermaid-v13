### #456 review fixes (done on `feature/456_packet-layout-fixes`, apart from the two #460 items)

- [x] **Spec: positions do not depend on measurements.** Lay out the same model under two measurers (e.g. `tableMeasurer` and a scaled one) and assert equal piece boxes, label centres, bit-number points and title point. `layout.spec.ts`, every fixture; a bit-number `y` that reads the descent fails 13 of them.
- [x] **Spec: title below the last row, as a box.** `invariants.ts` checks the first line's midpoint; check the title box's top against the last row's bottom, so a large `packetTitleFontSize` fails it. The box check failed `tiny` (1 px rows) and a 60 px title, so the layout changed too. Orchestrator choice to record: a title line taller than the row pitch moves down to sit on the last row, the one position a measurement moves (output changes only where v12 overlapped the rows).
- [x] **Spec: D343 always reaches the floor.** The shrink loop stops after `FIT_STEPS = 4`; with a non-linear measurer a label can stop above 10 px and still overflow. - [x] **Standards: quadratic invariant.** `coverViolations` filters every piece per field (O(fields × pieces), > 10,000 pieces in the corpus); group pieces by field once. One pass into a `Map`; `isWholeField` does not fit the cover check (it compares one piece, the check walks a field's pieces).
