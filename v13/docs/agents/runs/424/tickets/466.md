## Drawing
- W = `sectionWidth` in cells (200 px → 25). Cards one cell in from the column sides, no row between cards or after the last; text a space in from the card border, wrapped at W − 8.
- Last row: ticket left (plain text), assignee right, each line flush right (as the SVG's `text-anchor="end"`; a list stays a block); stacked when `ticket.w + assignee.w ≥ card − 4`.
- Priority: `┃` with `┎`/`┖` (Very High, High), `┆` (Low, Very Low); ASCII `I` / `:` plus the bracketed tag on the top border `+-[!!]---+` (`[!!]`, `[!]`, `[-]`, `[--]`).
- `ASCII_TOO_WIDE` comes from the API (not folded). Warnings: `KANBAN_PRIORITY` (as SVG), `ASCII_DROPPED` `style` / `emphasis` / `link`.
