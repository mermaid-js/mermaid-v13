# #198: label of an edge between a group and its own member

Branch `feature/198_layered-group-label` (spec #251).

## What changed

- `packages/core/src/layout/layered/compound.ts`
  - :692 `ownOf`, `runsDown`, `ownOffset`: the group ↔ own-member edges grouped by group. In
    px a labelled one now runs straight down from the group's top into the member's top, as it
    already did on the grid. Unlabelled px edges keep their old centre-to-centre line.
  - :968 level layout: a group with such labelled edges gets a band of label rows below its
    title (one label a row; px adds `loopGap` per label; on the grid the band uses the top
    padding rows first, so a single label adds no row). The box widens, still centred, until
    the labels fit right of the lines and of the lines entering through the top border.
  - :1227 routes: px starts on the top border, or below the title when the title is in the
    way (centred title, `minW − 2·padX` wide). On the grid the route is unchanged (`freeTop`).
  - :1252 labels: each sits in its band row, right of every line of that group
    (`ownGapX`: half a cell / `max(endLabelAcross, portPitch/2)` in px, which clears ER marks),
    pushed past any vertical route through its row (`clearOf`, `verticalsAcross` :455, :462).
- New spec `packages/core/src/layout/layered/group-member-labels.spec.ts`: 24 cases, px and
  cells × flowchart- and ER-shaped graphs × TB/LR × (group → member, member → group with a wide
  member, two such edges in a nested group). Checks validateLayout, label inside the frame
  (off the border cells on the grid), clear of title, every node, the other labels, and of every
  route. All 24 failed before the fix (overlaps member; lines through labels).
- `packages/mermaid/src/ascii-frames.spec.ts`: 3 public-API ASCII cases (flowchart one and two
  labels, ER) check every frame row keeps both borders and each label is drawn whole. Red
  before (the label overwrote the right border; `a label` and `back` overlapped).

## Oracle

v12 12.2.0 accepts all three inputs (flowchart-v2, flowchart-v2, er). The oracle is parse-only,
so it has nothing to say about where the labels go.

## Changed snapshots / goldens

None. No snapshot or golden changed: the flowchart/ER/class ASCII goldens, the corpus layout
crossing snapshot and `layered.spec.ts.snap` all came out byte-identical.

One assertion changed: `end-labels.spec.ts` "known limit" case (grid fixture F4, which has this
exact shape, plus synthetic start/end labels): the member end's end label used to land above
the group's top border and now lands on the border row (`y < top + 1`, was `< top + 0.5`). It
still clears every node, label and title (validateGrid is clean). Only class diagrams use end
labels, and they have no group ↔ member edges, so no real input reaches this.

## Unmet / not done

- E2E not run here. No e2e fixture has this case (unchanged corpus snapshots); it's left for
  the final full run.
- In px an unlabelled group ↔ member edge still draws centre to centre (inside the member).
  That's a separate defect, left alone to stay in scope.
- Lines that cross the band on the grid get no extra rerouting: the label moves past them instead.
