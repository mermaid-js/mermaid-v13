# #42 — layered engine reads LayoutGraph.metrics and honours the grid quantum

Branch `feature/42_m2-engine-metrics`. D77, D78, D140, R-ARCH-3, R-ASCII-1, DESIGN §6.3.

## What changed
- Every length constant in `layered.ts`/`compound.ts`/`index.ts` reads `graph.metrics ?? pxProfile.engine`
  (`LayeredOptions.metrics`); `availableWidth` turns at `metrics.turnBreakpoint`. `LayoutMetrics` unchanged.
- `LayoutGraph.grid` (`LayeredOptions.grid`): box edges on the grid, lines (ends, bends, port nodes,
  tracks) on cell middles, gaps rounded up, tight scaling kept whole. Each end on a side gets its own
  cell off the corners, `portPitch` apart (else 1). D140: a side with too many ends (or none free of
  corners) grows and `layered` runs again (≤ 3 passes); the model reports grown sizes, same centre.
- Grid self-loops (`gridLoop`): out of one side and back into it `portPitch` further on (so a loop
  side needs 5 cells), `loop` cells out; groups keep loop room in their parent (`CNode.loopW/H`).
- Group→own-member edges: straight down from the frame into a free top cell (repair is #45's).

## Model contract with `grid` (doc on `LayoutGraph.grid`)
Coordinates are cell indices. Route point `(c, r)` = cell; a box covers columns `x − w/2 + ½ … x + w/2 − ½`.
Route ends are the cell just outside their side; collinear and duplicate points are dropped.
