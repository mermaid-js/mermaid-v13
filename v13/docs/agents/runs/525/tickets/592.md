# T0c #592: ELK maps `LayoutEdge.sides` to fixed-side ports (D503)

Branch `feature/592_elk_sides` (worktree `arch-t592`), based on `origin/mermaid-v13` @ 2161a3047 (still the tip), head d397c7e82. Own PR into `mermaid-v13`, not pushed. `pnpm check` green (1,248 files, 23,874 tests).

## Exports

- No new export, and nothing in core. `toElkGraph` (`layout-elk/src/adapter.ts`, `sidePorts`) gives each named end under ELK layered its own port. It sets `FIXED_SIDE`, or `FIXED_POS` on a node with `anchorY` that takes a `left`/`right` end. `readModel` (`portsOf`) reports the named sides in `ports`.

## Behaviour (for T3/T4 with `layout: elk`)

- Each named end leaves or enters by its side, square to it. `left`/`right` ends on a node with `anchorY` (architecture services and junctions) sit on the icon line, and `top`/`bottom` ends at the side's middle. Ends with no side are ELK's.
- Only `elk` / `elk.layered` read sides. The other ELK algorithms ignore ports and are unchanged.
- `border` (`{group}`) is not read, as in `layered` (GAPS § T0c).
