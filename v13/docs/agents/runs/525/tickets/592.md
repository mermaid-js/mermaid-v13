## Exports

- No new export, and nothing in core. All the code is in `layout-elk/src/adapter.ts`:
  - `endSides` decides the side each end meets.
  - `sidePorts` builds the ports, with `FIXED_SIDE`, or `FIXED_POS` (`fixedAt`) on an `anchorY` node with a `left`/`right` end.
  - `checkAnchors` refuses `anchorY` as `layered` does.
  - `titleRoom` lays the graph out a second time for a titled group with ports.
  - `portsOf` reports the named sides in `ports`.

## Behaviour (for T3/T4 with `layout: elk`)

- Each named end leaves or enters by its side, square to it. Ends with no side are ELK's, except on an `anchorY` node (architecture services and junctions) that takes a `left`/`right` end:
  - Every end on it gets a port. A free end takes the flow's side.
  - `left`/`right` ends sit on the icon line when alone on their side, else spread `spacing.edge` apart about it.
  - `top`/`bottom` ends spread across the side.
- Only `elk` / `elk.layered` read sides. Edges between a group and its own member, and `border` (`{group}`), are not read (GAPS § T0c).
