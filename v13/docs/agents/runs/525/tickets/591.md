## Behaviour (for T3/T4 with `layout: layered`)

- Ranks, node order and boxes are layered's own; nodes may only all shift together when a stub reaches past the drawing (the drawing grows).
- An end whose route does not already use its named side gets a straight stub out of it (`max(portSep, max(headRoom, marker.length) + corner)`), then right angles round its own box to the route's middle points, which keep the label. `ports` report the named sides.
- Every end on a side that gains a moved end is re-spread along it (`portPitch`, round the middle, or round `anchorY` on `left`/`right`), ordered by where each heads.
- Self-loops with sides are drawn round the box between the named sides, label beside the outer leg. Group ends follow sides; an edge between a group and its own member keeps layered's route.
- Grid (`grid: 1`, ASCII): sides ignored. Unknown side: `RangeError`, as `compass`.
