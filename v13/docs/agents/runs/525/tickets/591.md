## Behaviour (for T3/T4 with `layout: layered`)

- Ranks, node order, boxes and the label are layered's. Only an end whose route does not already use its named side moves; `ports` report the named sides.
- A moved end sits at the side's middle (`anchorY` down a `left`/`right` side). Its stub runs straight out for `corner + headRoom + marker length`. Right angles then join the route's middle: along the stub first where the route lies ahead, else across to a lane `stub` past the box's edge.
- A 2-point route gives both ends stubs. New points are clamped to half the padding inside the drawing.
- Self-loops and group ends go through the same path. Edges between a group and its own member are not special-cased.
