## Deviations from the ticket text (escalate)

- The ticket summarises v12 as "`clusterBkg` in redux; `mainBkg` and `#f0f0f0` in classic".
  v12 differs per theme; these are v12's values (D151 oracle). In `redux-dark` and
  `redux-dark-color` v12 has `#16141f`, not `clusterBkg` (`#1e1a2e`); `dark`, `neutral` and
  `base` have `#555`, `#f4f4f4` and `tertiaryColor`, not `#f0f0f0`. Redux values are literals
  as in v12, so they do not follow a `clusterBkg` override.
- Not added: v12's `compositeBackground` (the body). The renderer draws it with `background`,
  which matches v12 except on `redux-dark` / `redux-dark-color` (v12 `#16141f`, v13 `#333333`).
  A third variable needs a maintainer decision.
