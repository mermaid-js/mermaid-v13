# #152 T0 edge-end labels (core, D102)

Branch `feature/152_edge-end-labels` (base `mermaid-js/mermaid-v13`, own PR there per D112), head 05e91ead6. No class code.

## Exports (`@mermaid-js/core/layout`)

- `LayoutEdge.startLabel?: Size`, `LayoutEdge.endLabel?: Size`: measured sizes, including any room the diagram wants around them (pad for a big source-side marker, such as a diamond, here).
- `LayoutModelEdge.startLabel?: Point`, `LayoutModelEdge.endLabel?: Point`: the centres.
- `LayoutMetrics.endLabelAlong` (px 4, cell 0) and `endLabelAcross` (px 6, cell 1).

## Placement (`layered/index.ts`, `endLabels`, run after the routes and grid titles are final)

- An end label sits beside the segment that leaves its port side. It starts `endLabelAlong` out (the target end adds `headRoom`) and sits `endLabelAcross` off the line, preferring the side the route does not turn towards.
- It avoids every label placed so far: all middle labels, and the end labels of edges placed earlier, in `graph.edges` order. If its preferred side is blocked it takes the other side. If both are blocked it moves out along the segment, past whatever is in the way, on whichever side needs less.
- It never moves onto a node or a group title. If both sides would, it falls back to the preferred spot, which `validateLayout` then flags (none in the sweeps).
- On the grid everything is whole cells.
- Room (`roomForEnds`, `layered.ts`): the stretch of gaps next to a node grows to hold the sum of all end labels on that side, so a fan's labels can stack. Each label adds its size along the flow plus `endLabelAlong`, plus `headRoom` at a head. Port edges whose inner end is the real end count too, which covers edges into group members.
- A graph with no end labels returns before any new work. No snapshot or golden changed.

## Validation

`validateLayout` reports these end-label problems: a missing or non-finite position; an end label over any node; over its own edge's labels; over any other edge's middle or end labels. `validateGrid` also reports end labels off the grid.

## Test helpers (`layered/end-labels.spec.ts`)

- `labelled(graph, start, end)` adds both labels to every edge.
- `endProblems(graph, model, near, stack)` checks a label is within `near` of its end, plus `stack` for each other end on the same node side, and off its edge's nodes.
- The sweeps cover:
  - `handBuiltFixtures` under curved, stepped and straight routing;
  - `cellFixtures`, `randomCellFixtures(60)` and `randomGroupFixtures(120)`.
- Per-direction specs cover TB/BT/LR/RL in px and cells, plus three labelled edges out of and into one node side in all four directions, px and cells.

## Known limits

- **Same-rank edges:** they can't occur. `orient` puts every edge from a lower rank to a higher one (minLen ≥ 1), so the `rb <= ra` guard in `roomForEnds` is only defensive.
- **Edges drawn without bends:** past `MAX_DUMMIES` (20,000) bends an edge runs straight and gets no reserved room. Placement still keeps its labels clear. The spec "edge drawn without bends" has minLen 30000; such an edge spans many ranks, so room is plentiful.
- **Grid edges between a group and its own member** (fixture F4): the route runs from the group's top border, outside the layered gaps, so no room is reserved. The labels stay clear of nodes, labels and titles, but the member end's label can land above the border, outside the group. A spec asserts this, and the cell sweep skips F4.
- **Group titles:** both modes treat them as walls, so no end label sits on a title. The rest of a group's border row is not a wall.
- **Reserved room:** it is the sum for a whole node side. That over-reserves when a fan's labels would fit side by side, and gaps grow only in graphs with end labels. T3 may tune this.
- **Other edges' lines** can still run through an end label, as in v12.
- **Self-loops:** the room `reserveLoop` keeps is not grown for end labels. The sweeps pass without it.
- **`units.spec`:** a per-key minimum (`MIN_CELL`) lets the cell `endLabelAlong` be 0.
