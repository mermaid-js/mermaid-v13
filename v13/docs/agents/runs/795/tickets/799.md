# #799 T3: ER row ink and the classic relationship label fill (done)

**What:** two ER table variables, `erRowOddTextColor` and `erRowEvenTextColor` (aliases `eot`, `eet`; `packages/diagram-er/src/theme/{vocabulary,table}.ts`). Rule `contrast(rowX, nodeTextColor, contrast(rowX, textColor, background))`: the node ink where it meets AA on the row (so an author's `nodeTextColor` still applies), else the better of the two inks `nodeTextColor` picks between, judged on the row. The header keeps `nodeTextColor`. The classic family rule `erEdgeLabelBackground: alpha(tertiaryColor, 50%)` (v12 `.labelBkg`); redux and neo keep `inline(linkLabelBkg)`, so the colour themes keep their own value. No theme-name check (D152).

**Renderer** (`render/render.ts`): the row `<g>` carries `fill` = the row ink, which the cells inherit, only where the row has a fill of its own (`theme.value(rowX) !== theme.value('mainBkg')`) and no author or palette fill. In redux and neo the rows are `mainBkg`, so the variable is never read: redux/neo ER output is byte-identical (checked by rendering all 228 ER fixtures under the six redux/neo themes before and after; the only diffs are three fixtures whose front matter pins a classic theme).

**Classic output changes:** every classic ER render with rows gains `fill="var(--_eot)"`/`var(--_eet)` on row groups and the two aliases; labels change fill to `tertiaryColor` at 0.5 (`#f9ffec80` default, `#201f1f80` dark, …). Argos will show the label tint in the classic baselines.
