Pele, in @mermaid-js/diagram-agentflow
--------------------------------------

The CDN build also bundles @mermaid-js/diagram-agentflow without its legal comments. That
package's parser/lexer.ts, parser/parser.ts, parser/base.ts, parser/statements.ts,
parser/model.ts, layout/prepare.ts, layout/shapes.ts, render/shapes.ts and render/edges.ts are
local copies of @mermaid-js/diagram-flowchart's lexer, parser, builder, shapes and edges (D446,
D467), which are ported from, or based on, Pele (https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele) at commit
3a2f075 (release 1.13.0). Pele is used under the MIT License:
