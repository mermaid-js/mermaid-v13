Pele, in @mermaid-js/diagram-c4
-------------------------------

The CDN build also bundles @mermaid-js/diagram-c4 without its legal comments. That package's
layout/grid.ts, layout/layout.ts, layout/metrics.ts, layout/path.ts, layout/route.ts and
layout/shapes.ts are ported from Pele's C4 grid, relation router and label placement, and its
render/shapes.ts draws C4's shapes with the shared shapes' paths from @mermaid-js/shared (D446,
D559), all ported from, or based on, Pele (https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele) at commit
3a2f075 (release 1.13.0). Pele is used under the MIT License:
